Can positions reveal hidden physical state?

PHAST observes only a recent position history. A causal observer infers a phase state; a port-Hamiltonian transition then rolls it forward while keeping storage, mass geometry, dissipation, and ports explicit.

What is guaranteed—and what is not?

  • Guaranteed: continuous-time port-Hamiltonian passivity under the stated assumptions.
  • Implemented: a structure-preserving split approximation, not an unconditional finite-step energy theorem.
  • Recovery: conditional on the declared information contract; position-only components are not generally identifiable.
