HNN, pHNN, and PHAST expose a learned Hamiltonian. pHNN and PHAST also expose PSD dissipation. Their Hamiltonians are learned up to scale and gauge, so the page does not compare raw energy magnitudes across models.

The visual comparison therefore uses the common object all methods predict: the held-out physical trajectory.

03Forecasting and physical identification remain different questions

Low rollout error shows that the learned transition predicts the observed system. It does not prove that every learned $V$, $M$, or $D$ component is uniquely identifiable from positions.

PHAST's recovery claims remain conditional on declared anchors and bounds.
