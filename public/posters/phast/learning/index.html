The four equations used by the experiments

Stage Equation Role
Observe $\hat p_t=\mathcal C_\psi\!\left(q_t,\mathcal O_\phi(q_{t-9:t})\right)$ Infer the unobserved momentum from recent positions.
Store $H_\theta(q,p)=V_\theta(q)+\tfrac12p^\top M_\theta(q)^{-1}p$ Separate potential and kinetic energy.
Evolve $\dot x=(J-R_\theta)\nabla H_\theta(x)+G_\theta(q)u$ Combine conservative exchange, irreversible loss, and applied effort.
Account $y^{\mathrm{port}}=G_\theta^\top\nabla H_\theta$$\dot H=-\nabla H^\top R_\theta\nabla H+u^\top y^{\mathrm{port}}$ Expose dissipated power and power entering through the port.

What the symbols mean

$q$
observed generalized position, such as an angle or cart location
$\hat p$
momentum inferred by observer $\mathcal O_\phi$ and canonicalizer $\mathcal C_\psi$
$x=(q,p)$
the phase state advanced after the observation window
$V_\theta,\ M_\theta$
potential energy and positive-definite mass or inertia
$J,\ R_\theta$
skew interconnection and positive-semidefinite dissipation
$G_\theta u$
where generalized effort enters; the animated forecasts use $u=0$
$y^{\mathrm{port}}$
the output conjugate to $u$, so $u^\top y^{\mathrm{port}}$ is supplied power

What structure changes in each example.

01Position-dependent dissipation

02Passivity is a native statement only for structured models

HNN, pHNN, and PHAST expose a learned Hamiltonian. pHNN and PHAST also expose PSD dissipation. Their Hamiltonians are learned up to scale and gauge, so the page does not compare raw energy magnitudes across models.

The visual comparison therefore uses the common object all methods predict: the held-out physical trajectory.

03Forecasting and physical identification remain different questions

Low rollout error shows that the learned transition predicts the observed system. It does not prove that every learned $V$, $M$, or $D$ component is uniquely identifiable from positions.

PHAST's recovery claims remain conditional on declared anchors and bounds.
