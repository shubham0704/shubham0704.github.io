1 · Learning problem

First define what is observed, learned, and tested.

The notation below follows the paper. It stays fixed when we move from forecasting to physical recovery and scaling.

ObserveTrajectories
$$\left\{q^{(b)}_{0:T-1}\right\}_{b=1}^{N_{\mathrm{train}}}$$

Each training trajectory contains $T$ observed configurations. $N_{\mathrm{train}}$ is the number of trajectories available for learning.

PredictOpen loop
$$\begin{aligned} \hat x_t&=\operatorname{Init}(\mathcal O_t),\\ \hat x_{t+h+1}&=\Phi_{\Delta t}^{\mathcal K}(\hat x_{t+h};u_{t+h}),\\ h&=0,\ldots,H-1 \end{aligned}$$

Under position-only sensing, $\mathcal O_t=q_{t-K+1:t}$. $\operatorname{Init}$ produces $\hat x_t=(\hat q_t,\hat p_t)$; $\Phi_{\Delta t}^{\mathcal K}$ advances it by one sample under the declared physics contract $\mathcal K$. These autonomous rollouts use $u=0$.

LearnOne step
$$\mathcal L_q=\frac{1}{T-1}\sum_{t=0}^{T-2}\operatorname{err}_{\mathcal Q}(\hat q_{t+1},q_{t+1})$$

The main q-only comparison trains every model with the same manifold-aware next-step observation loss. Ground-truth damping is not a training target.

TestTwo questions
$$\operatorname{RollErr}_{\mathcal Q}(H)\ \downarrow \qquad\text{and}\qquad R_D^2\ \uparrow$$

Open-loop error asks whether the future is predicted. Damping recovery asks whether the model identified the physical loss law. They need not improve together.

$H$

rollout horizon

$H_\theta(x)$

Hamiltonian, or learned total energy

$N_{\mathrm{train}}$

number of training trajectories

PHAST realization

PHAST gives the learned transition a phase state and an energy account.

The observer implements $\operatorname{Init}$. The port-Hamiltonian dynamics implement $\Phi_{\Delta t}^{\mathcal K}$. The equations below specify what that transition is allowed to store, exchange, dissipate, and receive through a port.

$$\dot x=(J-R_\theta)\nabla H_\theta+G_\theta u$$
$$\dot H_\theta=-\nabla H_\theta^\top R_\theta\nabla H_\theta+u^\top y^{\mathrm{port}}$$

Dissipation

$R_\theta$ makes irreversible loss explicit.

Its mechanical block $D_\theta(q)\succeq0$ removes energy without being allowed to create it. This is the quantity tested in the recovery study.

$P_{\mathrm{loss}}=\nabla H_\theta^\top R_\theta\nabla H_\theta\geq0$

Read the observer and pH symbols

From observations to state

$\hat p_t=\mathcal C_\psi\!\left(q_t,\mathcal O_\phi(q_{t-9:t})\right)$ infers the unobserved momentum from recent positions.

$H_\theta(q,p)=V_\theta(q)+\tfrac12p^\top M_\theta(q)^{-1}p$ separates potential and kinetic energy.

$x=(q,p)$ is then advanced by the pH transition above.

$y^{\mathrm{port}}=G_\theta^\top\nabla H_\theta$ is conjugate to $u$, so $u^\top y^{\mathrm{port}}$ is supplied power.

What UNKNOWN means

PHAST-UNKNOWN receives the position history and the declared coordinate chart. It receives no potential, mass, damping, port map, velocity, momentum, or simulator phase state.

4 · Single-axis diagnostics

What changes when motion, capacity, or training time changes?

These pilot comparisons vary one factor at a time. Starting motion changes only the pendulum's initial momentum: scale 0.35 versus 4.0; the plant and damping law stay fixed. The corrected joint $N\times T$ scaling study follows in Section 5.

Forecast$$\operatorname{RollErr}_{\mathcal Q}(H)=\frac{1}{BH}\sum_{b=1}^{B}\sum_{h=1}^{H}e_{\mathcal Q}(\hat q_{b,K+h-1},q_{b,K+h-1})$$lower is better
Recover damping$$R_D^2=1-\frac{\sum_q\lVert D_\theta(q)-D^\star(q)\rVert_F^2}{\sum_q\lVert D^\star(q)-\bar D^\star\rVert_F^2}$$higher is better

$B$ is the number of held-out trajectories and $e_{\mathcal Q}$ is the same coordinate-aware error used in training. $D^\star$ is the simulator damping law, $\bar D^\star$ its mean over test configurations, and $D_\theta$ the learned law. For $R_D^2$, zero matches a constant mean-damping predictor; a negative value is worse.

6 · Research frontier

What the evidence supports, and what the thesis must still resolve.

Supported

Limitation

Forecasting is not unique physical identification.

Bounded damping removes one demonstrated error sink. It does not prove that $V$, $M$, and $D$ are uniquely recoverable from positions without declared anchors.

Next experiment

The proposed machinery begins after the diagnostic boundary

  1. Identify: determine whether residual evidence identifies a changed mechanism or only an observational ambiguity.
  2. Adapt: protect established structure while updating only justified physical directions.
  3. Act: correct the model from action-conditioned observations without destabilizing the physical loop.
