1 · Model

Two equations turn a forecast into a physical account.

The state equation says how the system moves. The power balance says where its energy goes.

$$\dot x=(J-R_\theta)\nabla H_\theta+G_\theta u$$
$$\dot H_\theta=-\nabla H_\theta^\top R_\theta\nabla H_\theta+u^\top y^{\mathrm{port}}$$

Dissipation

$R_\theta$ makes irreversible loss explicit.

Its mechanical block $D_\theta(q)\succeq0$ removes energy without being allowed to create it. This is the quantity tested in the recovery study.

$P_{\mathrm{loss}}=\nabla H_\theta^\top R_\theta\nabla H_\theta\geq0$

Read the observer, symbols, and exact rollout contract

From observations to state

$\hat p_t=\mathcal C_\psi\!\left(q_t,\mathcal O_\phi(q_{t-9:t})\right)$ infers the unobserved momentum from recent positions.

$H_\theta(q,p)=V_\theta(q)+\tfrac12p^\top M_\theta(q)^{-1}p$ separates potential and kinetic energy.

$x=(q,p)$ is then advanced by the pH transition above.

$y^{\mathrm{port}}=G_\theta^\top\nabla H_\theta$ is conjugate to $u$, so $u^\top y^{\mathrm{port}}$ is supplied power.

What UNKNOWN means

PHAST-UNKNOWN receives the position history and the declared coordinate chart. It receives no potential, mass, damping, port map, velocity, momentum, or simulator phase state.

5 · Research frontier

What the evidence supports, and what the thesis must still resolve.

Supported

Limitation

Forecasting is not unique physical identification.

Bounded damping removes one demonstrated error sink. It does not prove that $V$, $M$, and $D$ are uniquely recoverable from positions without declared anchors.

Next experiment

Three questions opened by this study

  1. Mechanism: which single PHAST component causes each measured gain under a matched ablation?
  2. Scale: what happens as trajectories lengthen, physical dimension grows, or sensors change?
  3. Action: when do typed forecasts improve repeated closed-loop decisions rather than prediction alone?
