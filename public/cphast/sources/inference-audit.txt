WHY IMPROVEMENT DOES NOT MEAN FULL RECOVERY
The triangle hides c=(1,-1,1): B_triangle*c=0. In the parallel graph,
B_parallel*c=(1,-1,0), so perturbing that same original direction changes
voltage history. However, (0,1,-1) is a new hidden parallel-line circulation.
The original target overlaps this hidden direction. With the frozen isotropic
0.15-A prior SD, its posterior variance cannot fall below 0.015 A^2, even with
perfect measurements. This is an expected-risk floor, not a lower bound on a
finite sample's empirical MSE. The best tested risk, 0.01549403, respects it.
Thus the original direction is detectable, but the target is not exactly
identifiable when the other initial currents are also unknown.
