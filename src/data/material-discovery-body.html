ABX3 adaptive material discovery · research progress

Can a learned policy find novel materials with target properties at lower first-principles cost?

We study a discovery policy that learns where to look. A recurrent HNet/PNet controller searches a 643-dimensional continuous space of five-site chemistry and lattice geometry, updates itself between episodes from live PBEsol-relax/TB09 measurements, and reuses audited observations as navigation anchors. Two co-primary tests measure it: the first-principles compute needed to reach a withheld target property, and the number of novel validated candidates returned when no target is given. The first now has evidence; the second is registered and built but not yet run.

Current empirical stateKnown-target comparison complete
v81sealed production identity

Fifty of fifty matched runs completed under one contract (arrays 964362 and 964363; four baseline runs resumed as 972268, 972271, 972274). The lineage audit found 0 of 2,500 charged evaluations scored outside the domain, and analysis job 972658 produced the authenticated package. The proposed method recovers both withheld targets in every seed at the lowest DFT compute of any arm; DARWIN alone keeps pace on call count; waypoint memory is a null result at this search length. The open-ended registry is frozen and its chain built; no open-ended run has executed.

Research questions

Three questions define the study.

Q1 and Q2 each have a registered experiment and failure condition. Q3 does not yet: no arm in the current matrix varies response supervision. See Frontier 02.

  1. Q1 · NavigationDoes the policy improve over episodes?

    Measure DFT compute to a withheld oracle-consistent target.

  2. Q2 · DiscoveryDoes it find better unknown candidates?

    Measure unique, feasible, independently validated ABX3 yield.

  3. Q3 · Physical supervisionDo first-principles responses improve learning?

    Test PBEsol structural and family-constrained APDFT energy tangents.

  1. 22 May 2026Closed-loop control
    01complete

    Closed-loop control became executable

    ProblemThe repository could rank candidates, but it could not demonstrate that physical measurements changed the controller used in the next episode.

    Construction
    HNet trajectories, PNet dynamics, live ABINIT labels, learning updates, and provenance-gated waypoint reuse first executed as one recurrent loop.
    Observation
    Live ABINIT HNet smoke plus checkpointed rollout and oracle traces.
    Inference
    The system could update its search policy from physically evaluated candidates rather than only rank a static replay set.
    Limitation
    This was a bounded smoke experiment, not the final PBEsol-relax/TB09 protocol or a multi-seed baseline comparison.

    02complete

    ProblemEarly proxy baselines could not support a defensible comparison because candidate domains, oracle access, and charged compute were not guaranteed to match.

  3. 13 Jul 2026Physical supervision
    03complete

    Physical supervision became native and family-constrained

    ProblemChemical and structural losses lacked audited first-principles response targets along the actual family-constrained coordinates used by discovery.

    Construction
    ABINIT PBEsol began supplying variational stress and native APDFT total-energy tangents along allowed Sn-Pb and halide-family simplex directions.
    Observation
    Three representative family directions passed centered-response checks with Richardson errors no larger than 5.99 x 10^-7 eV per simplex coordinate.
    Inference
    HNet can receive audited structural and chemical stability directions instead of invented or zero-filled derivatives.
    Limitation
    These are PBEsol total-energy responses. They are not TB09 band-gap derivatives and are presently anchored at registered task starts.

  4. 11 Aug 2026Production oracle
    04complete

    The composite oracle became production-fast

    ProblemA faithful relax-then-gap observation was correct but too slow and operationally fragile for a matched multi-method campaign.

    Construction
    Candidate evaluation was locked to PBEsol internal-coordinate relaxation followed by TB09 band-gap measurement, then scaled across 18 MPI ranks.
    Observation
    Six of six ABX3 family references reproduced; CsSnI3 latency fell from 628.6 s to 73.8 s with an unchanged 1.142878 eV gap.
    Inference
    The selected targets and starts can be evaluated consistently at 8.52x lower wall-clock latency.
    Limitation
    Validation covers the registered ABX3 family cases and profiles, not arbitrary compounds, cells, or exchange-correlation choices.

  5. 16-18 Aug 2026Production admission
    05complete

    The experiment became production-admissible

    ProblemThe acquisition score lacked a device-bound certificate, while four release attempts exposed incomplete overlays, API skew, device mismatch, and CPU pooling failures before oracle execution.

    Construction
    A 216/108 calibration split licensed total uncertainty, and a sealed release bound the complete package, runtime, models, pseudopotentials, tasks, methods, seeds, and run specifications.
    Observation
    All 324 oracle cases passed; held-out coverage was 0.6759/0.9444/0.9907; independent release hashes and runtime checks authenticated the launch.
    Inference
    The complete 50-run comparison could execute under one uncertainty, software, oracle, and accounting identity.
    Limitation
    Authorization proves what is running, not whether the proposed method wins. The first two sealed campaigns were later voided: their acquisition scores were computed on lattices outside the task domain (see the next milestone).

  6. 28 Aug - 3 Sep 2026Domain admissibility
    06complete

    Proposals became admissible by construction

    ProblemThe differentiable rollout used an unbounded log-lattice chart, so the acquisition score could be computed on a geometry the oracle never evaluated. A lineage audit found 49.4 and 50.6 percent of the hits in the two earlier sealed campaigns were selected that way; both campaigns were voided.

    Construction
    A bounded arctan chart replaced the unbounded parameterization, the derivative supervision was corrected to the chart's Jacobian (a 26x to 2,152x error near the walls), every configuration declares its admissible domain, and out-of-domain decoding now fails closed instead of clipping.
    Observation
    Lineage audit over all 2,500 charged evaluations of the corrected campaign: 0 candidates scored outside the task-resolved domain, 0 contaminated hits.
    Inference
    The geometry scored by the acquisition function is the geometry the oracle evaluates, which is the precondition for any comparison between arms.
    Limitation
    The audit verifies the HNet arms, whose rollouts carry the defect; baselines have no continuous relaxation and are inapplicable rather than verified. Efficiency against the voided campaigns cannot be compared because those campaigns are not valid evidence.
    Inspectexperiments/results/known_target_recovery/production_v1/campaign_v81_50runs/audit/v81_audit_50.json
  7. 3-6 Sep 2026Known-target result
    07complete

    The matched recovery comparison completed

    ProblemNo sealed campaign had reached all 50 registered cells: one lost a run to a non-finite rollout, the next lost four to the 48-hour wall.

    Construction
    The corrected campaign completed 50 of 50 runs: 20 HNet runs in under three hours each, 30 baseline runs including four resumed from their case caches after the wall, then the registered analyzer produced the authenticated package.
    Observation
    Proposed method recovers both withheld targets in 10 of 10 runs (first recovery at 2.0 [1.0, 4.0] charged calls on the halide task, 12.2 [3.2, 29.2] on the B-site task) at 86.5 [80.7, 91.9] DFT core-hours per run against 113.4 for DARWIN, 113.2 for random search, and 231.3 for kernel-greedy; paired differences favor it against random search on normalized AUC and calls, against kernel-greedy on calls and compute, and against DARWIN on compute only.
    Inference
    The controlled-navigation claim has evidence: the method is the fastest locator and the cheapest in first-principles compute among the registered arms, and DARWIN is the one baseline that keeps pace on call count.
    Limitation
    Under this oracle only: no spin-orbit coupling, a five-atom cell, a 0.10 eV tolerance finer than the model error. Disabling waypoint memory changed no registered quantity, so memory is a null result at this search length. The baseline arms ran without the evaluator deadline the HNet arms had; it never bound on HNet and a capped-compute sensitivity preserves every compute difference.

03 · Co-primary empirical program

Two empirical tracks under live DFT

Target recovery tests navigation. Open-ended search tests discovery.

Track A · Known-target recovery: oracle calls and core-hours to a withheld target.Track B · Open-ended material discovery: unique validated yield without a reference structure. Both policies remain adaptive across measured episodes.

Decision rule. Support requires a stable paired advantage after charging failures, retries, and derivative costs; the memory claim additionally requires an advantage over the no-memory arm. A null result is no stable paired advantage, no episode-wise learning trend, or no memory benefit.

04 · Open frontiersRemaining evidence

What is not yet known

Each frontier states what is demonstrated, what must be built, and the result that would close it. A frontier can fail its own gate.

Frontier 01resolved

Can a differentiable search policy stay inside the domain its surrogate was calibrated on?

Move from clipping out-of-domain proposals after the fact toward dynamics that never leave the calibrated region.

Open target
The geometry scored by the acquisition function is the geometry evaluated by the oracle.
Current evidence
Resolved. The bounded arctan chart with its Jacobian in the derivative supervision, the fail-closed decode, and the per-configuration domain declaration produced 0 of 2,500 charged evaluations scored outside the domain in the completed campaign, against 49.4 and 50.6 percent contaminated hits in the two voided ones.
Research advance required
Carry the chart and the audit as acceptance gates into the open-ended track; the audit is the gate every campaign must pass before its results support a claim.
Completion gate
Met on the admissibility half: zero out-of-domain scoring. The efficiency half could not be compared to the unconstrained policy, whose campaigns are not valid evidence.
Established problem

Constrained and manifold-aware optimization, trust-region methods, and reparameterized bounded search are standard. Surrogate-model literature likewise warns against querying outside the calibration envelope.

Our question goes further. The narrower question was whether enforcing the constraint inside the dynamics preserves search efficiency; the constrained policy recovers every target within a handful of calls, so nothing suggests it cost efficiency, but no matched unconstrained comparison exists.

Evidence boundary. The gradient blow-up that voided the earlier campaigns was fixed at the chart, not isolated to a component; the fix removes the mechanism rather than diagnosing it.

Frontier 02unregistered

Does measured first-principles response actually improve the learned policy?

Move from verifying that physical tangents are numerically correct toward showing that they change search behaviour.

Open target
A stable paired advantage attributable to response supervision rather than to the rest of the loop.
Current evidence
Three representative family directions passed centered-response checks with Richardson errors no larger than 5.99 x 10^-7 eV per simplex coordinate. The completed campaign's memory ablation was null, which says nothing about response supervision because both arms enabled it identically.
Research advance required
Register a response-supervision ablation. No current matrix varies the response term.
Completion gate
A sixth arm identical to the proposed method with response supervision disabled shows a reproducible paired difference in core-hours to first hit, or the question is retired and the claim withdrawn.
Established problem

Derivative-informed surrogates and gradient-enhanced regression report sample-efficiency gains, generally under analytic or cheaply available derivatives.

Our question goes further. Here the derivative is an expensive, family-constrained first-principles response supplied as detached supervision across a stop-gradient measurement boundary. Whether that buys search efficiency at live-DFT cost is untested.

Evidence boundary. Numerical validity of the tangents is demonstrated. Their effect on discovery performance is entirely unmeasured, and no registered experiment currently varies them.

Frontier 03registry frozen

Does the policy discover useful candidates when no target is given?

Move from recovering a withheld target toward yielding unique, feasible, independently validated materials.

Open target
Unique validated ABX3 yield at matched charged-call and core-hour budgets, with no hidden target structure.
Current evidence
The registry is frozen after four external review cycles and the chain is built and merged: a 102-phase stability release builder with same-method static energies, a species-aware novelty classifier against named OQMD and JARVIS snapshots, two validation profiles, a global-box initial design, the stability channel through HNet and every baseline, a versioned compiler, and an analyzer whose admission, identity, cost, and censoring rules are frozen as registry values. No open-ended run has executed.
Research advance required
Seal after the final chain workstream and review; run a one-node real-oracle smoke of the unexercised paths; compute the reference release and the 648-case calibration panel; then seed 0 as an operational gate and seeds 1-4 under an unchanged contract.
Completion gate
Every method searches the registered domain under one accounting contract; shortlisted candidates are confirmed by an independent tighter-TB09 rerun with a drift gate; admitted discoveries are novel against the named snapshots and inside the registered hull's 0.10 eV/atom screen.
Established problem

Generative and active-learning pipelines for inorganic materials commonly report screened candidate counts, typically against fixed surrogates rather than a live first-principles oracle inside the loop.

Our question goes further. The question is whether a policy that updates from measured physics between episodes returns a better validated set than matched baselines at equal physical cost, rather than a larger unvalidated shortlist.

Evidence boundary. This is a co-primary manuscript claim with no evidence yet. Its result will be stated as gap-confirmed, search-profile-screened discoveries under the registered model: stability is screened at the search profile and assessed by cell relaxation, not confirmed under tighter numerics; novelty is relative to named snapshots, not an undocumented pretraining set.
